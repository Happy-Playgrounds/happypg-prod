<h2>A Complete Turf System Replacement</h2>

Over time, artificial turf systems can wear down, lose their appearance, and create uneven surfaces that impact both safety and aesthetics. Tulsa CAP Eugene Field's existing turf had reached the point where it was time for a complete replacement.

The scope of this project included:
- Removal and disposal of all existing turf and aging materials
- Re-grading and leveling of the site to restore a smooth, uniform surface
- Installation of new rock base material where needed
- Placement of 1-inch impact-absorbing padding throughout the area
- Installation, seaming, and finishing of brand-new artificial turf

Every step had to be carefully coordinated to ensure the project remained on schedule while maintaining the high standards our clients expect.

<h2>Working Against the Clock</h2>

School construction schedules often come with unique challenges, and this project was no exception. With the first day of school rapidly approaching, every day mattered.

Our crews worked efficiently to remove the old materials, prepare the subgrade, and install the new turf system without compromising quality. Careful planning, experienced installation teams, and strong project coordination allowed us to stay ahead of schedule throughout the process.

The result was a complete transformation completed with days to spare before students and staff returned to campus.

<h2>A Safe, Clean, and Beautiful Finished Product</h2>

The finished installation dramatically improved the appearance and functionality of the outdoor space. The new turf provides a clean, vibrant look while the 1-inch pad below offers additional comfort and safety for children using the area each day.

The refreshed surface creates an inviting environment for learning, play, and outdoor activities while giving the school a durable solution that will serve students for years to come.

Looking across the completed project, it's hard to believe how much was accomplished in such a short timeframe. The lush green turf now provides a beautiful, uniform finish that enhances the entire campus and creates a welcoming space for students as they begin another school year.
